Learning Games

Pick a game. Each one keeps track of how it’s going, and has a “grown-ups” panel behind a little math gate.

  • Deep Dive Math

    Every answer takes the submarine deeper. The math gets harder as the water gets darker, and there’s a creature waiting in each zone.

    Dive in
    Adding & subtracting Place value

  • Letter Train

    Load the right letter into the train. It starts with the letters in his own name and adds new ones as those get easy.

    All aboard
    Letter recognition Letter names

  • Coin Shop

    Silly things for sale. Pay the exact amount out of pennies, nickels, dimes and quarters — then learn to do it with the fewest coins.

    Go shopping
    Counting money Adding to a total
